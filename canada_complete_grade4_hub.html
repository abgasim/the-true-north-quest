🧠 Best Memory Tricks for 4th Graders

Fourth graders remember geography best when using **West-to-East order**, **catchy word associations**, and **silly pictures**!

🧭 1. The Clockwise Compass Trick

N - E - S - W → "Never Eat Soggy Waffles!" or "Never Eat Slimy Worms!"

Go clockwise starting from the top: North (up), East (right), South (down), West (left).

🍁 2. West-to-East Province Order Mnemonic

Travel across Canada from the Pacific Ocean to Ontario:

British Columbia • Alberta • Saskatchewan • Manitoba • Ontario
"Big Apples Smell Mighty Ok!"

💡 3. Silly Associations for Capital Cities

Link the sound of the province to something memorable in the capital:

British Columbia → Victoria
"Queen Victoria sailed to the British coast."
Alberta → Edmonton
"Albert's best friend is Ed (Ed-monton)!"
Saskatchewan → Regina
"Sasquatch had fun in Regina."
Manitoba → Winnipeg
"The Manic Toby dog loves to Win (Winnipeg)!"
Ontario → Toronto
"On to Toronto!"
Nunavut → Iqaluit
"It's None-of-it (Nunavut) warm in an Igloo (Iqaluit)!"
Yukon → Whitehorse
"Ride You-can ride a White horse in the snow!"
Northwest Territories → Yellowknife
"Head Northwest with a shiny Yellow knife!"
