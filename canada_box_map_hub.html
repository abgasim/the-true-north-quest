🧠 Memory Hacks for the Box Map

Use these simple rules to easily memorize the diagram[cite: 1, 2, 19]:

2. The 5 Western & Central Provinces (Bottom Left to Right)

BC ➔ AB ➔ SK ➔ MB ➔ ON ➔ QC[cite: 19]

• Top Shelf: Newfoundland & Labrador (NL)[cite: 19]

• Bottom Floor: New Brunswick (NB) on the left, Nova Scotia (NS) on the right[cite: 19]

"New dogs (NL) love Potatoes (PE) and Nice Seashores (NB & NS)!"
