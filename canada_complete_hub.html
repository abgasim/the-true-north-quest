Document 2: Cardinal Directions & Canadian Geography

The 4 cardinal directions are North (N), East (E), South (S), and West (W). A common mnemonic device to remember their clockwise order is: Never Eat Soggy Waffles[cite: 2].

Canada's Geographic Boundaries:

  • Canada is South of the Arctic Ocean[cite: 10].
  • Canada is East of the Pacific Ocean[cite: 10].
  • Canada is West of the Atlantic Ocean[cite: 10].
  • Canada is North of the United States[cite: 10].

Document 3: Population Density in Canada

Population Density measures how many people live in a specific unit of area:
Population Density = Total Population ÷ Land Size in km²
