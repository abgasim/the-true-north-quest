🧭 The 4 Cardinal Directions

N E S W Compass

Mnemonic: Never Eat Soggy Waffles (North, East, South, West)

  • North: Canada borders the Arctic Ocean.
  • South: Canada shares a border with the United States.
  • East: Bordered by the Atlantic Ocean.
  • West: Bordered by the Pacific Ocean.

👥 Population Density Visualizer

Population Density = How many people live in a specific amount of space (people per km²).

Low Density (e.g., Nunavut)

Lots of open land, few people.

Medium Density (e.g., Saskatchewan)

Towns and farming communities.

High Density (e.g., PEI / Toronto)

Many people packed close together.
